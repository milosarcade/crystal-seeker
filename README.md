# Crystal Seeker

Mine ore against the clock. When the whistle blows, whatever is in your
satchel is sold at the assay office, and you spend the cash on better gear.

Play it: https://milosarcade.com/crystal-seeker/

## How to play

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| W A S D or arrows | Move |
| Walk into rock | Mine it |
| Space | Swing at what you face |
| Space or Enter | Start a shift |
| Enter | Next shift, from the assay office |

- Each shift is timed. It starts at 20 seconds and a better lantern adds more.
- Ore prices are re-quoted every shift, so check the board before you go down.
- Rarer ore sits further from where you start.

## Upgrades

Pickaxe, Grip, Boots, Satchel, Lantern, Lucky strike and Survey map. Buy out
every upgrade in a mine to unlock the next one.

## Mines

1. Copper Hollow
2. Frostbite Cave
3. Magma Vault
